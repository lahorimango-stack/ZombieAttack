3. FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters)
----------------------------------------------------
The night is long, the street is blocked, and they are coming.

Drag your finger to draw a line of weapons, then release to throw them straight into the horde. Aim well, because every throw counts and the dead do not stop.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag to create weapons in any formation
• Time slows down while you draw, so plan your shot
• Release to throw
• Kill every zombie before your health runs out

25 LEVELS
Fight from a quiet street to a burning hell gate and beyond. Each level has its own place, sky, weather and enemy mix: a crawling night, a hound hunt, a thunderstorm, a toxic zone and more. Difficulty rises and falls, so tense battles are followed by a moment to breathe.

ZOMBIES THAT KEEP YOU MOVING
• Runners sprint straight at you
• Zombie dogs hunt in fast, low packs
• Crawlers come in low, then stand up
• Bombers explode, so kill them from far away
• Brutes take a lot of punishment
• Mini-boss: The Butcher
• Final boss: King of the Dead

9 WEAPONS TO UNLOCK AND UPGRADE
Knife, Axe, Crystal Spear, Grenade, Ripper Blade, Blast Kunai, Storm Orb, Rocket and Cluster Bomb. Bounce blades between zombies, stick explosive kunai into them, or chain lightning through the horde. Upgrade every weapon and your armor in the Armory.

SMASH THE STREET
Shoot explosive barrels and cars to wipe out whole groups in one blast. Wooden barricades, crates and tires break apart when you hit them.

CINEMATIC FINAL KILLS
The last kill plays in slow motion from dramatic camera angles.

Contains blood and zombie violence.
