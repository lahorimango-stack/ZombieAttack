3. FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters)
----------------------------------------------------
The night is long, the street is blocked, and they are coming.

Drag your finger to draw a line of weapons, then release to throw them straight into the horde. Aim well, because every throw counts and the dead do not stop.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag to create weapons in any formation
• Time slows down while you draw, so plan your shot
• Release to throw
• Kill every zombie before your health runs out

10 LEVELS, 10 BATTLEGROUNDS
Fight from a quiet street to a burning hell gate. Each level has its own place, props and wave design. Difficulty rises and falls, so tense battles are followed by a moment to breathe.

ZOMBIES THAT KEEP YOU MOVING
• Runners sprint straight at you
• Crawlers come in low, then stand up
• Bombers explode, so kill them from far away
• Brutes take a lot of punishment
• Mini-boss: The Butcher
• Final boss: King of the Dead

6 WEAPONS TO UNLOCK
Knife, Axe, Crystal Spear, Grenade, Fire Bomb and Rocket. Each one has its own damage, speed and area.

USE THE STREET
Shoot explosive barrels and cars to wipe out whole groups in one blast.

CINEMATIC FINAL KILLS
The last kill plays in slow motion from dramatic camera angles.

Contains blood and zombie violence.
